# Video Generation Prompt

Generate a **photorealistic cinematic video** for the hero section of the website page **"Рубленные дома" (Handcrafted Log Homes)**.

## CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

This must be a house built using **Canadian Log Construction (Canadian Saddle Notch / Canadian Log Joinery)**.

This is **NOT** a house made of profiled timber, glued laminated timber, square timber, rectangular timber, or any modern milled wood.

The house must be constructed from **large natural round logs** using authentic **Canadian saddle notch corner joints**.

The video is intended **only** for the page about **handcrafted log homes**.

---

# Construction Requirements

The walls must be made from:

* massive natural round logs
* full-diameter tree trunks
* hand-peeled logs
* visible natural taper of the logs
* irregular organic wood texture
* handcrafted appearance
* authentic Canadian log construction

The logs must **NOT** have:

* flat sides
* square profile
* rectangular profile
* machined timber appearance
* laminated timber look
* profiled timber grooves
* modern modular wood construction

Every log should remain naturally round.

---

# Canadian Corner Joinery

The corners are the most important element.

Use authentic:

* Canadian Saddle Notch
* handcrafted log joinery
* interlocking round logs
* deep saddle notches
* extended log ends beyond the corners
* traditional Canadian craftsmanship

The corner joints should immediately communicate that this is **Canadian log construction**, not Scandinavian timber construction.
