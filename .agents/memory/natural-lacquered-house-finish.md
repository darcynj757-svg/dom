---
name: Natural lacquered house finish
description: Agreed final appearance of the scroll-driven house model.
---

The completed house should keep its natural wood color and texture, with a restrained glossy lacquer highlight; do not turn the facade white.

**Why:** The natural timber reads as more authentic for the construction story, while a lower roughness in the final orbit communicates finished protective coating without losing the material identity.

**How to apply:** Animate a moderate roughness reduction only during the final section of the orbit, and leave the dark roof, windows, and other contrast details unchanged.
