---
name: Natural lacquered house finish
description: Agreed final appearance of the scroll-driven house model.
---

The completed house should keep its natural wood color and texture, with a restrained glossy lacquer highlight and cool moving glints on the glass; do not turn the facade white.

**Why:** The natural timber reads as more authentic for the construction story, while a lower roughness and a subtle warm sweep communicate finished protective coating without losing the material identity. Separate cool highlights make the glazing read as real glass.

**How to apply:** Animate a moderate roughness reduction and warm lacquer sweep on wood only during the final section of the orbit; keep the dark roof and frames unchanged, while glass may receive a restrained cool reflection sweep.
